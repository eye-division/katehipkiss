CURRENT AND UPCOMING:


Out of Paper 
Falmouth Art Gallery, The Moor, Falmouth TR11 2RT
4th Oct 2025 - 24th Jan 2026
This captivating exhibition explores how paper is used as a medium and the many forms it can take. Created in collaboration with Falmouth School of Art, Falmouth University. 

Wells Art Contemporary
Wells Cathedral, Somerset
8th Oct - 1st Nov
An international exhibition in the stunning setting of Wells Cathedral. My altered atlas, Home, will be on show.

East Oxford Art Collective Winter Market
Greyfriars Church Hall, Iffley Road, Oxford OX4 1SB
Sat 15th Nov 10am-4pm

Made in Oxford Christmas Market
Caper bookshop, Magdalen Road, Oxford, OX4 1RE
Sun 23rd November, 10am-4pm.

Modern Makers Winter Market
Yattendon Village Hall, Church Lane, Yattendon, RG18 0UE
29th - 30th Nov
Modern Makers Collective's annual celebration of handmade excellence. I will be exhibiting a collection of layered papercuts alongside a wonderful group of talented makers. Join us for some festive inspiration and cheer!
