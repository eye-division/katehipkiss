CURRENT AND UPCOMING:


Out of Paper 
Falmouth Art Gallery, The Moor, Falmouth TR11 2RT
4th Oct 2025 - 24th Jan 2026
This captivating exhibition explores how paper is used as a medium and the many forms it can take. Created in collaboration with Falmouth School of Art, Falmouth University. 

Fresh Art Fair with Kingfisher Art Gallery
Alexandra Palace, London
30th Jan - 1st Feb 2026
A selection of my layered papercuts will be exhibited.

Artweeks Exhibition with East Oxford Art Collective
Greyfriars Church Hall, Iffley Road, Oxford
16th - 17th May 10am - 4pm
23rd - 25th May 10am - 4pm
12 artists exhibiting a wonderful collection of work ranging from glass, prntmaking, jewellery, painting, sculpture and textiles. You are welcome to drop in to browse, buy or chat!
