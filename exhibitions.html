CURRENT AND UPCOMING:

RWA Paper Works Biennial
Royal West of Englad Academy, Queen’s Road, Bristol BS8 1PX
25th Jan - 27th April 2025
I am delighted to have had 2 pieces of work selected for this exciting exhibition which will celebrate works on and from paper.

The Gallery at Waterperry Gardens
Near Wheatley, Oxfordshire, England. OX33 1JZ
A selection of my layered papercuts are on display at this beautiful gallery located in an 18th century barn.

Dividing Lines
North Wall Art Centre
1st-19th April 2025
Group exhibition exploring Oxford as a place to live from multiple viewpoints.

Artweeks Exhibition
Jericho, Oxford
3rd-5th and 8th-11th May 2025
I'm looking forward to once again exhibiting my work alongside Glass Artist Wendy Newhofer and Jeweller Becky Morgans. I will post further details closer to the time and you can sign up to my mailing list if you'd like to receive details to you inbox.
