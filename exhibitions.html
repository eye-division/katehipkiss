CURRENT AND UPCOMING:


Royal Academy Summer Exhibition
18th June - 18th August 2024
I'm delighted that my altered book sculpture Coming Home has been selected for the Summer Exhibition this year.

RWA171 Annual Open Exhibition
14th Sept 2024 - 5th Jan 2025
A Way To Go has been selected for this year's exhibition held at the RWA in Bristol. This is always a beautifully curated exhibition and I'm thrilled to be part of it.

OCG Group Exhibition
Oxford Town Hall, St Aldates, OX1 1BX
9th - 14th Sept, 10am-5pm
I'm looking forward to exhibiting a collection of layered landscape and architectural papercuts at this group show of 10 Oxfordshire Craft Guild Members. Including ceramics, glass, jewellery and textiles and coinciding with the Saturday of Oxford Open Doors.

Dividing Lines
North Wall Art Centre
1st-19th April 2025
Group exhibition exploring Oxford as a place to live from multiple viewpoints.
